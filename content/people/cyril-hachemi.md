Cyril joined the group as a Postdoctoral Research Associate (PDRA) in January 2026, where he investigates how electrolyte concentration influences the properties of the electrode-electrolyte interfaces and, ultimately, electrochemical activity.

He completed his MSc by Research at Deakin University (Australia) in 2022, where he focused on understanding the impact of microplastics on membrane processes. Then, he obtained his PhD from Lyon University (France) in 2025, exploring novel titanium-based photoelectrodes for solar-driven water oxidation. During his PhD, he combined the development of innovative oxide and oxysulfide photoelectrodes with synchrotron-based characterisation techniques, particularly NAP-XPS and RPES, to investigate semiconductor-liquid interfaces.

In his spare time, Cyril enjoys spending time with his family. He also runs and regularly goes to the gym. Outside sports, he likes playing the guitar and didgeridoo.
