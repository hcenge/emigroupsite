After growing up in the sunny South Wales valleys, Liam studied physics at University College London (with a year abroad in Seattle), before embarking on a PhD in physics at the University of Cambridge focusing on the structural and electronic properties of battery cathode materials, finishing in 2024. Following his PhD, Liam went to California for a postdoc at the Stanford Synchrotron Radiation Lightsource, using X-ray radiation to study materials for green hydrogen production and novel magnetic applications. At present, he is a postdoc in the Weatherup Group in Oxford, studying next-generation battery cathode materials.

Outside of work, Liam enjoys adventures and reading.
