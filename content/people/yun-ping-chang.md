Yun-Ping joined the Weatherup Group as a DPhil student in 2026. Her research focuses on understanding the interfacial reactions between cathodes and electrolytes in batteries. She received her bachelor's degree in Chemistry from National Taiwan University, where she conducted undergraduate research on the redox mechanisms of halide cathode materials for all-solid-state batteries. Outside of research, she enjoys watching films and travelling.
